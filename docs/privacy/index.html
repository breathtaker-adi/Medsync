Privacy Policy & Data Charter

MedSync is engineered around a simple premise: your medical data belongs to you alone. We implement strict on-device encryption, zero third-party telemetry, and full alignment with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

01 / Introduction & Core Philosophy

MedSync ("we", "our", or "the platform") is an open-source medical schedule and adherence management platform. Traditional health applications funnel sensitive patient information into centralized cloud warehouses for behavioral profiling, monetization, or insurance telemetry. MedSync repudiates this paradigm.

We adhere strictly to data minimization and purposeful processing as established under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023, Republic of India). All patient records remain sovereign property of the data principal.

02 / Categories of Data We Process

To provide offline schedule reminders and dosage validation, MedSync processes the following limited information:

  • Prescription Documents & OCR Imagery: Images captured via the in-app camera to identify medication names, strengths, and dosage intervals. OCR extraction executes locally on your hardware. Images are never uploaded to public AI training corpuses.
  • Adherence Telemetry: Timestamps when doses are marked as taken, snoozed, or missed.
  • Caregiver Linkage Data: Pseudonymized device identifiers and cryptographic tokens used exclusively to route push notifications when critical doses are unlogged.
  • Practitioner Signatures: Digital confirmation signatures verifying prescription modifications by registered medical practitioners.

03 / Cryptographic Security & Storage Architecture

Your personal health data is guarded with defense-in-depth:

  • At Rest: Stored locally using SQLCipher with AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption. The encryption key is derived using PBKDF2 with 250,000 iterations and protected by device biometric hardware (Android Keystore / Secure Enclave).
  • In Transit: Synchronized peer-to-peer or over encrypted TLS 1.3 / WebSocket channels with mutual certificate authentication.
  • Zero Third-Party SDKs: We do not embed Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Mixpanel, Crashlytics, or any ad telemetry brokers in the application.

04 / The Doctor Care Loop & Clinical Safety

In strict accordance with Indian medical ethics guidelines and clinical safety norms, MedSync does not permit automated prescription changes without human confirmation:

CLINICAL SAFETY MANDATEAI transcription is treated purely as a draft recommendation. Medication schedules generated from scanned prescriptions require explicit verification and sign-off by a verified medical practitioner before dosage intervals become binding in the timeline.

05 / Your Rights as a Data Principal (DPDP Act 2023)

Under Chapter III of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you hold enforceable rights regarding your health data:

Right to Access (Section 11)

You can inspect all recorded health data at any time and export a standardized JSON ledger with one click.

Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12)

You have the unconditional right to correct inaccurate records or trigger complete cryptographic deletion of all local and synced data.

Right to Nominate (Section 14)

You may designate a nominee (family member or legal guardian) to manage your health timeline in the event of death or incapacity.

Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13)

Direct access to our designated Data Protection Officer with mandatory response timelines under Indian law.

06 / Data Retention & Permanent Shredding

Because MedSync prioritizes on-device storage, data retention is entirely governed by the patient. If you uninstall the application, all local SQLCipher database files and cryptographic key rings are immediately purged by the operating system sandbox.

To manually purge all logs without uninstalling, open Settings in MedSync and select Purge Local Health Ledger.
