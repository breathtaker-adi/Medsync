01 / Data Fiduciary & Core Philosophy

MedSync ("we", "our", or "the platform") operates as an open-source medication adherence and caregiver safety platform. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023, Republic of India), MedSync acts as the Data Fiduciary regarding the personal health data processed within the application.

We adhere strictly to purpose limitation and data minimization. We do not sell, broker, or monetize your health data, nor do we build behavioral profiles for insurance or commercial advertising purposes.

02 / Categories of Data We Process & How It Works

To deliver medication alarms, caregiver alerts, and prescription digitization, MedSync processes the following categories of data:

  • Prescription Documents & AI OCR: When you snap a prescription photo, the image is transmitted securely over TLS 1.3 to Supabase Storage and parsed by our serverless AI Vision edge function to extract medication names, dosage strengths, and schedules. Images are stored securely in your private storage bucket and queued for clinician review before schedules become active. Informed consent is gathered prior to your first scan.
  • Medication Schedules & Adherence Logs: Timestamps when doses are logged as taken, missed, or snoozed, along with medication inventory counts.
  • Caregiver & Doctor Pairings: Cryptographic 8-character pairing codes with 15-minute expiry, status indicators, and permission links between patients, family caregivers, and verified clinicians.
  • Device Push Tokens: FCM and Expo notification tokens used strictly to dispatch urgent missed-dose notifications and refill alerts.

03 / Cloud Architecture, Storage & Sub-processors

MedSync uses a defense-in-depth architecture combining cloud security with on-device hardware protections:

  • In Transit: All communications between the mobile application and backend services are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • At Rest (Cloud): Stored in an encrypted Supabase PostgreSQL database protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. Patients can only query their own records, caregivers can only inspect actively linked dependents, and doctors can only access patients under clinical care.
  • At Rest (Device): Authentication session tokens are stored using expo-secure-store, backed by the hardware-isolated Android Keystore and iOS Keychain.
  • Zero Advertising SDKs: We never integrate Google AdMob, Facebook Pixel, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer, or data brokers.

05 / Your Rights as a Data Principal (DPDP Act 2023)

Under Chapter III of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you retain enforceable rights:

Right to Access (Section 11)

Inspect all recorded medication data at any time and export a standardized machine-readable JSON health report.

Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12)

Correct inaccurate health records or trigger complete deletion of your account, database records, and prescription images.

Right to Nominate (Section 14)

Designate a family caregiver or legal guardian to access and manage your adherence schedule in the event of incapacity.

Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13)

Direct grievance redressal through our designated security channel with resolution procedures under Indian law.

06 / Data Retention, Uninstall vs. Account Deletion

It is important to understand the difference between uninstalling the mobile app and deleting your account:

  • Uninstalling the App: Removes local app sandbox storage, cached schedules, and hardware keychain tokens from your physical phone. However, encrypted records in Supabase remain associated with your registered account so your schedule is preserved if you reinstall.
  • Deleting Your Account (Permanent Erasure): To permanently purge all records from both your phone and cloud servers under Section 12 of the DPDP Act 2023, navigate to Settings → Delete Account. This triggers our database procedure (delete_user_account) which cascades deletion across prescriptions, logs, paired links, push tokens, and storage buckets.
